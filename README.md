# 💫 About Me

👋 **Hey, I'm Emma**

Content creator, builder of questionable devices, and professional overthinker of fun ideas.  
I travel, I make stuff, and I record the chaos so others can learn (or at least laugh).

---

### 🎥 What I Put on the Internet
- Videos of **projects, hackathons, travels, experiments**  
- Tech explained the way I wish someone had explained it to me  
- Robots misbehaving, AI doing its best, and behind-the-scenes from the things I host with **Hack Club**  
- YouTube → [**@Ametista0_0**](https://www.youtube.com/@Ametista0_0)

---

### 🛠️ What I Build
- 🤖 Attitude-filled robots  
- 🧠 Brain–computer experiments *“for science”*  
- 🎮 Tiny games + AR effects  
- 🔌 Hardware/software combinations that probably void a warranty  

---

### 🚀 Meanwhile…
- I run hackathons (yes, even that Moonshot one at NASA)  
- I document everything on **[emmaborghi.tech](https://emmaborghi.tech)**  

If a project works on the first try, I get suspicious ✨
